# جت‌سئو نیوز (JetSEO News)

A Chrome extension for news editors and journalists, by [JetSEO](https://www.jetseo.ir). A side panel (or a full page) in five sections: «امروز» (the dashboard), «سوژه‌ها» (hot stories, the coverage gap, Telegram, multimedia, foreign media), «ترندها» (Google, Wikipedia, occasions), «کلمات پرجستجو» (searched phrases) and «سایت ما» (our articles); in the side panel the open section's lists are in a second row; the full page shows every list in one column beside the content, the sections as headings (above the content in a window narrower than 760px). «تنظیمات» is a button in the header. The lists:
- **سوژه‌های داغ:** stories that several Iranian news sites are telling right now. Each shows how many Telegram channels told it too, «اول در تلگرام» when a channel was ahead of the sites, and a «ترند» badge when it matches a Google or Wikipedia trend. «نوشتن با هوش مصنوعی» copies a prompt for an AI assistant: read the story's articles, answer what readers ask, and write three headlines, a lead and the article. The button has one name and the prompt follows the story (the button's tooltip says which): an update when one of the team's sites already tells it (what to add or correct), a fresh angle on a coverage gap (one no competitor took), and on «رسانه‌های خارجی» a Persian article from the English ones. Once a prompt is copied, the card offers the same prompt with another output: a Telegram post, Google Discover headlines, or questions and answers.
- **رقبا زده‌اند، ما نه:** stories your competitors published and your site did not.
- **رسانه‌های خارجی:** stories several international (English) sources are telling. A story is marked «در رسانه فارسی دیده شد» when a Persian headline holds its Latin names («GPT», «OpenAI»); a story without the mark may still be told in Persian with every name in Persian letters. The headlines are English and left to right; everything around them is Persian. JetSEO translates nothing and asks no language model: every headline links to its article and has «ترجمه» (Google Translate's Persian page of that article, in a new tab). Only for tiers with «خبر خارجی» (the admin sets it per tier); other subscribers see what the tab is and the link to the plans.
  - On each story: «در سایت شما» when one of the team's own sites has it, «N منبع تازه» when sources are picking it up now (and the order «رو به رشد»), «کلیدواژه تحت نظر» for the team's or your own watched words (a Latin word such as `OpenAI` matches the English headlines), «مردم چه می‌پرسند؟» (what readers in Iran search along with the story's names, from Google's autocomplete), and «من می‌نویسم» / «رد برای تیم», which mark the story for the whole team: a taken story shows who is writing it, a dismissed one is hidden for everyone until «نمایش ردشده‌های تیم». The order «همه، حتی تک‌منبعی» also lists what one source alone told (science and health sites seldom tell the same story).
  - A watched word in a new foreign story is notified like the Persian ones.
- **Every story card** reads the same way: the headline opens the first source's article; the count of sources and at most two badges are in sight, the rest under «جزئیات» with the headlines; the main button («من می‌نویسم», the card's one filled button) and the other actions (مردم چه می‌پرسند؟، نوشتن با هوش مصنوعی، رد شد) beside it, all in sight. A click on a badge writes under it what it means (its tooltip). Under «جزئیات»: «دنبال کن» adds a word of the story to the member's watched words, and «نشان نده» beside a source mutes that site (a line at the foot of the page says so, with «برگردان»). After «نوشتن با هوش مصنوعی» copies its text, the card says where it goes (paste it into ChatGPT or any assistant).
- **The dashboard** («امروز») says at a glance what happens and what waits: «N از M سوژه داغ را زده‌اید», how many stories are heating up, «الان باید دید» (the stories nobody on the team has yet: a watched keyword first, then what the competitors told, then what is heating up, each with «من می‌نویسم» on it) and «تیم الان چه می‌نویسد» (who took what). In the hot stories' bars «✓» marks what a team's site has and «✍» what a member is writing. For an editor: a taken story still on none of the team's sites after 45 minutes is «معطل» (`STALLED_MINUTES`), «پوشش به تفکیک میز» counts the hot stories the team has per topic (the desk furthest behind first), and «کپی گزارش شیفت» copies the dashboard as text for the newsroom's group. In the full page the cards flow in columns, and a headline takes two lines at most.
- **«مقایسه با رسانه‌های دیگر»** on the dashboard ranks the sites by how many of the period's hot stories each told and how many it told first (among the sites), the 8 that told most and the team's own row («شما») wherever it stands; it is in «کپی گزارش شیفت» too. Counted in the extension from `topics` (`newsMediaRank`): a story lists its 12 earliest sites, so a site that came later to a bigger story is not counted for it (the card says in how many stories), and an own site whose feed is outside JetSEO's catalog has its coverage but no «بار اول».
- **The period** («خبرهای N ساعت اخیر») is one for the dashboard, the hot stories, the coverage gap, Telegram and multimedia (1 to 24 hours, `prefs.hours`): chosen on any of them, kept for all, and the coverage-gap alerts use it too. «رسانه‌های خارجی» keeps its own (up to 72 hours).
- **Every list** shows its period and order; its other choices are behind «فیلتر», whose number says how many are on. Under the hot stories a line says how many stories the team's filters and your own left out.
- **«جا مانده‌ها»** on the dashboard puts the three «what did I miss» numbers together: what the competitors told, what the foreign media told, and what only Telegram channels told.
- **«نقش من»:** the first time a member with the module opens the extension (nothing of theirs in `chrome.storage.sync` yet), it asks what they do: خبرنگار، دبیر، سردبیر، چندرسانه‌ای or «همه امکانات» (`ROLES` in `panel.js`). The role sets which lists are on (`prefs.hiddenTabs`) and the «سوژه بی‌صاحب» alert, once; «همه امکانات» hides none. It can be changed in «تنظیمات», where every list can still be switched on and off by itself. For a reporter and the multimedia desk the dashboard begins with «الان باید دید» and says its numbers in one line; the tiles, the desks, the media ranking, the publishing speed and «کپی گزارش شیفت» are the editors'.
- In «تنظیمات» the lists you do not use can be switched off. Watched and excluded words and muted sites are chips, added one at a time; every change there is saved as it is made. «خروج» is there too; the header's «صفحه کامل» / «پنل کناری» button goes to the other view. After an update, «چه چیزی تازه است» says once what changed.
- **تلگرام:** stories only Telegram channels have told so far (no site yet), or the posts spreading fastest for their channel. The channels are JetSEO's catalog and those a team asked for: in the panel («سایت و رقبا») the subscription owner suggests public channels of the team's field, as with sites, and JetSEO's admin adds each to the catalog or for that team only.
- **چندرسانه‌ای:** for a multimedia desk, from the hot stories: those other sites made a video, photos, an infographic or audio for («کار شده»), and those at least 3 sites told with none yet («فرصت»). The server keeps no media type, so it is read from the headline's marker («فیلم/ …», «… + تصاویر») or the URL's section (`/video/`, `/photo/`, `/media/`); a headline without one is missed.
- **مطالب ما:** your site's articles of the last 24 hours, how many minutes after the first competitor each came out, «مقایسه تیتر» (the headlines other sites gave the same story, from the `topics` endpoint, and the words most of them use and yours does not), and a news SEO check of any of them (title, description, H1, canonical, robots, the image Discover needs, NewsArticle data, publish time, language).
- **ترند گوگل:** today's searches in Iran.
- **کلمات پرجستجو:** the member types a subject («یارانه», «لیگ برتر») and sees what readers search about it and how many times a month each phrase is searched, the most searched first. It is the panel's «کلمات کلیدی پرجستجو» for a newsroom: the phrases are Google's autocomplete for the subject alone and with each Persian question word («چیست», «چرا», «کی», …) or informational word («علت», «ماجرای», «نتیجه», …) before or after it, about 30 requests from the member's own browser, four at a time. «پرسشی و اطلاعاتی» (the default) keeps the questions and the informational searches; «همه» shows every phrase. The searches come from JetSEO (`keywords`); a phrase JetSEO has no volume for shows «—». With the module only.
- **خروجی فایل:** «ذخیره در اکسل» in the header saves the list on screen (after its filters and order) as a CSV file, UTF-8 with a BOM so Excel opens it in Persian. Every list has it except the settings. The dashboard's file is one sheet with a block per «بخش» (the numbers, the publishing speed, the whole media ranking, the desks, «الان باید دید», what the team writes, the open gaps, Google's trends), in the columns «بخش، عنوان، مقدار، توضیح، لینک». A cell that begins with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` gets a leading apostrophe, so a headline cannot run as a formula.
- **ترند ویکی‌پدیا:** Persian Wikipedia articles whose reads jumped yesterday (at least twice the day before and the week before), the most read, or the hot edits: articles several people edited in the last 3 hours, which is where a death or an event shows first. «انگلیسی، بدون فارسی» lists English Wikipedia's most-read articles of the day that have no Persian article yet.
- **مناسبت‌ها:** today, tomorrow or the day after from Wikipedia's day pages: events, births and deaths with how many years ago (round anniversaries first) and the world days.
- **تنظیمات:** your own filters and alerts. «فقط سوژه‌های همین کلیدواژه‌ها را نشان بده» keeps the dashboard, the hot stories, the coverage gap, Telegram, multimedia and the foreign media to the stories with one of your watched words (or the team's) in a headline, for a member who follows a few subjects only; the hot stories then also list what one site alone told. A line over those lists says which words, with «نمایش همه سوژه‌ها». The trends and «مطالب ما» stay as they are; with no word of your own it does nothing.

While Chrome is open, the extension checks every 5 minutes and notifies new coverage gaps and stories with a watched keyword (the team's, or the user's own from «تنظیمات»), also those only on Telegram. A notification's click opens the extension on that tab. «سوژه بی‌صاحب» (on for the subscription's owner, a member can switch it on in «تنظیمات»): a coverage gap or a watched story that nobody took and no team's site has, 30 minutes after this browser first saw it (`UNCLAIMED_MINUTES`), told once; its click opens the dashboard. A notification of one story nobody marked has a «من می‌نویسم» button: the story is read again and marked only while it is still free, and the notification then says what happened. «ساعت سکوت» in «تنظیمات» (from an hour to an hour of the computer's clock, over midnight too) tells nothing, and what came then is not told afterwards; the icon's number and the lists still have it.

Also:
- Hot stories not on the list the last time it was in sight are marked «جدید»; a search box filters the headlines.
- Every story has a topic badge (سیاسی، ورزشی، …, decided on the server from the feeds' sections and the headline). Chips over the hot stories, or «موضوع‌های من» in «تنظیمات», keep only your own desk's topics, out of the team's «حوزه خبری» set in the panel; a story with a watched keyword stays whatever its topic.
- «مردم چه می‌پرسند؟» on a story asks Google's autocomplete what readers search with it, and adds that to its «نوشتن با هوش مصنوعی».
- «رد شد» on a coverage gap hides that story from the list and the alerts, for the whole team.
- «من می‌نویسم» on a story tells the team you are writing it: every member sees «می‌نویسد: نام» on it; «منصرف شدم» frees it.
- A story one of the team's own sites already tells is marked «در سایت شما» (and «بدون آنچه زده‌ایم» over the hot stories leaves those out); «N منبع تازه» marks a story several sites picked up in the last hour, and «ترتیب: رو به رشد» puts those first.
- Right-click a page of one of the team's sites, or a link to one: «بررسی سئوی این خبر» opens «مطالب ما» with its news SEO check.
- A list drawn again (a mark, a filter, «تازه‌سازی», the 3-minute refresh) stays at the scroll position it was read at, with the «جزئیات» that were open; the refresh waits while the answers of «مردم چه می‌پرسند؟» are open on a card. «تنظیمات» has «راهنمای برچسب‌ها و دکمه‌ها», what each badge and button means.
- Alt+Shift+N opens the extension (changeable in `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`).
- On a list of stories: `J` / `K` the next / the last card (its headline gets the focus, `Enter` opens the article), `W` «من می‌نویسم» or «منصرف شدم», `/` the search box. By the key's place, so they work with the Persian layout on.
- A list that did not load for want of a connection says so, and is read again when the browser is back online.

The hot stories, the coverage gap, Telegram and the alerts come with the «جت‌سئو نیوز» module, bought in the JetSEO panel (`/panel/NewsModule`). There the subscription owner also manages team members, sites, competitors and team filters. Google Trends, Wikipedia trends and the occasions are free for every signed-in JetSEO user.

## Install (development)

1. Clone this repository, or unzip a release.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and switch on «Developer mode».
3. Click «Load unpacked» and choose the folder.
4. Click the extension's icon to open the side panel. Click «ورود به جت‌سئو», sign in on jetseo.ir, and come back.

Chrome 116 or later.

## Files

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `manifest.json` | MV3. The `key` fixes the extension id to `eooiikfbhffioefpapknekohmpningie`; jetseo.ir hands sign-in tokens only to known extension ids. |
| `api.js` | The account (sign-in hand-off, token), calls to the JetSEO API, personal preferences, and the text folding the server's filters use. |
| `background.js` | Finishes the sign-in when jetseo.ir's `/extension/connected` page opens. Every 5 minutes: alerts, the number of unseen gaps on the icon, and the right-click menu for the team's sites. Opens the app on a tab for a menu or notification click (`newsGo` in `storage.session`). |
| `panel.html`, `panel.css`, `panel.js` | The side panel. Every value from the server is escaped (`esc`); links must be `http(s)` (`safeUrl`). |
| `lib.js` | The panel's rules that need no page (the story prompts, a headline's media kind, a phrase's type, what a headline lacks), so `tools/check.js` can run them. Loaded between `api.js` and `panel.js`. |
| `fonts/` | Vazirmatn Regular and Bold (SIL Open Font License, `Vazirmatn-OFL.txt`). |
| `tools/check.js` | `node tools/check.js`: the checks of `api.js` and `lib.js` that need no browser (the CSV of «خروجی», the text folding, the alerts' lists, the quiet hours, the prompts). |
| `tools/pack.ps1` | Builds the release zip (forward-slash paths, as the Chrome Web Store requires). |

## How it talks to JetSEO

- **Sign-in:** the user signs in on `https://www.jetseo.ir`, which returns to `/extension/connected`. The extension then calls `POST /extension/token` with the site's cookie. The site answers only this extension's id (checked by `Origin`), and the extension keeps the token in `chrome.storage.local`.
- **API:** `https://coreapi.jetseo.ir/api/News/…`, with the token in the `X-JetSEO-User` header.
  - `state`: who you are for the module (owner, member or none), your tier and end date, and the team's sites.
  - `topics?hours=&minSources=`: hot stories, with the team filters already applied.
  - `gap?siteId=&hours=`: a site's coverage gap.
  - `alerts?hours=`: what a round of the alerts reads, in one answer: `gaps` (per site with a feed and competitors), `topics` and `telegram` (the lists a watched word is looked for in) and `foreign` (null without it). A server without it answers 404, and the lists are then asked one by one (`newsAlertLists`).
  - `mine?siteId=&hours=`: the site's own articles, with each one's lag behind the first competitor.
  - `check?url=`: news SEO check of one article, for the team's own sites only.
  - `telegram?mode=only|trend&hours=`: stories only channels told, or the fastest-spreading posts.
  - `foreign?hours=&category=`: international stories, each with `covers`: the Persian headlines found for it, if any. 403 `{reason: "no-foreign"}` when the tier does not include it; `state.foreignNews` says so beforehand, and a server without that field hides the tab.
  - `POST mark?storyId=&status=taken|dismissed|none`: the team's mark on a story: «Take» and «Dismiss» in «Global», «رد شد» on a coverage gap (held for the whole team).
  - `POST keywords` with a JSON array of phrases (at most 300): `{keywords: [{keyword, volume, informational}]}` for those JetSEO has a monthly search volume for («کلمات پرجستجو»). A server without it answers 404: the phrases then show without their searches.
  - Free, signed in only: `google`, `wiki?mode=rising|top|edits|missing`, `days?offset=0..2`.
  - A 401 means signed out; the extension forgets the token. A 403 `{reason: "no-module"}` means no subscription; only Google Trends, Wikipedia trends and the occasions stay.
- **Google's autocomplete** (`suggestqueries.google.com`, the «مردم چه می‌پرسند؟» and «کلمات پرجستجو»): read directly, since Google Search is not filtered in Iran. An optional host permission, asked on the first «مردم چه می‌پرسند؟» click or «کلمات پرجستجو» search: a new required one would disable the extension on update until each user approves it.
- **Use counts:** `POST used?what=&category=` tells JetSEO that the «Global» list was opened (once a half hour), a foreign article was opened or sent to Google Translate, «Angles» was asked, or «انگلیسی، بدون فارسی» was opened; and for the Persian module tabs, that a tab was opened (once a half hour each) or a headline was opened from one. Only the action and the list's category are sent, never the story or its address. JetSEO's admins see the totals of each team.
- **Nothing else.** Google Trends, Wikipedia and Telegram are filtered in Iran, so the extension never reads them itself: CoreApi and JetSEO's news loop read them through ai.jetseo.ir (design doc 11-7 in the JetSEO repository).

## Release

1. Raise `version` in `manifest.json`.
2. Run `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools/pack.ps1`. It writes `JetseoNewsExtension-<version>.zip` next to the repository, its manifest without `key` (the store refuses it).
3. **Chrome Web Store:** to keep the same id, the first upload of a new item must carry the private key of the manifest's `key`: `tools/pack.ps1 -Key <path to the .pem>` adds it as `key.pem`. That key is kept outside this repository, never committed (see `.gitignore`); later uploads go without it.
   - If the store assigns a different id, add `chrome-extension://<id>` to jetseo.ir's appSetting `ExtensionAllowedOrigins`, or sign-in will be refused.
